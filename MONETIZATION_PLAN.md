# Custos no-subscription monetization plan

## Decision

Keep Custos free to use with no mandatory subscription and no sale of ledger data.

Monetize the trust and attention around the product instead:

1. Tips and sponsorships to cover operating costs.
2. A one-time Lifetime Supporter purchase for optional perks.
3. Clearly disclosed, privacy-safe house ads and affiliate offers outside the encrypted ledger.
4. Fixed-price B2B work: self-hosting setup, private deployments, workshops, and custom privacy engineering.

The core ledger, encryption, exports, and backups should remain available without a subscription.

## Non-negotiable privacy boundaries

- Never use transaction amounts, notes, categories, wallet names, addresses, or decrypted ledger content for targeting.
- Do not sell or license personal financial data.
- Do not place ad SDKs or targeted ads beside encrypted ledger content.
- Keep advertising and affiliate placements on the marketing site or a clearly separate Offers surface.
- Disclose affiliate relationships in plain language.
- Do not claim that advertising or research partners receive category totals until a real, privacy-reviewed pipeline exists.
- If aggregate sharing is ever considered, require explicit opt-in, a documented k-anonymity/re-identification design, a real buyer, and an independent privacy review first.

## Revenue ladder

### Stage 1 — cover costs

- Add a Support Custos CTA to the marketing site, README, Account menu, and selected empty states.
- Use Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, or an equivalent one-time tip provider.
- Prefer house sponsorships on the marketing site before third-party ad networks.
- Track monthly infrastructure, email, domain, and support costs.

Success means the official host runs without regular personal top-ups.

### Stage 2 — meaningful side income

Launch a one-time Lifetime Supporter SKU at approximately RM49–99 / US$12–25.

Keep the ledger free. Sell optional perks only:

- Supporter badge.
- Additional themes or accent colors.
- Early access to experiments.
- Higher reminder volume only if delivery costs become material.
- Supporter acknowledgements where the user explicitly opts in.

Do not gate encryption, exports, backups, or basic tracking. Existing users should not lose promised functionality.

### Stage 3 — full-time potential

Package fixed-price B2B work rather than introducing a mandatory consumer subscription:

- Paid self-hosting and deployment setup.
- Private hosted deployments.
- E2EE and wallet-authentication workshops.
- Custom privacy architecture or integrations.
- White-label work only after a qualified buyer requests it.
- Optional fixed-scope maintenance or support packages.

Potential buyers include privacy-focused developers, financial-wellness providers, universities, cooperatives, fintechs, NGOs, and employers serving independent workers.

## Illustrative $1M annual mix

This is a target model, not a forecast:

| Source | Volume | Annual revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lifetime Supporters | 10,000 × US$25 | US$250,000 |
| Self-hosting / setup projects | 20 × US$15,000 | US$300,000 |
| Workshops and custom privacy work | 15 × US$20,000 | US$300,000 |
| Sponsorships, affiliates, and tips | — | US$150,000 |
| **Total** |  | **US$1,000,000** |

The consumer side is primarily a distribution and cost-recovery engine. B2B projects and technical services must carry most of the path toward full-time income unless Custos achieves unusually large consumer reach.

## Product and website work

### Week 1–2

- Add Support Custos links to the marketing site, README, and Account menu.
- Keep the official host free with full core functionality.
- Align Terms, privacy, and consent copy with the actual implementation.
- Remove or rewrite claims about advertising/research partners receiving category totals if no such pipeline exists.
- Publish simple support and payment disclosures.

### Week 3–6

- Add a one-time payment link for Lifetime Supporter.
- Start with a manually managed or minimal webhook-backed entitlement.
- Implement only the badge/theme perk first.
- Measure visits, supporter conversion, payment geography, refunds, and support burden.

### Week 7–9

- Publish a separate Tools & Offers page.
- Add only two or three real affiliate relationships.
- Use generic, non-personalized placements; never choose an offer from a user's ledger.
- Add visible commission disclosures.

### Week 10–12

- Publish a one-page B2B offer for self-hosting, setup, workshops, and private deployments.
- Contact a small set of qualified organizations and developers.
- Sell a fixed-price pilot before building enterprise administration speculatively.
- Use the first paid engagement to identify what should become reusable product capability.

## Measurement

Track these without decrypting user ledgers:

- Marketing CTA clicks.
- Demo and app activation steps.
- Support CTA conversion.
- Lifetime Supporter conversion and refund rate.
- Monthly infrastructure and delivery costs.
- Affiliate clicks and confirmed commissions.
- B2B leads, proposals, paid pilots, delivery hours, and gross margin.
- Support volume and recovery-related incidents.

Do not optimize for raw ad impressions at the expense of privacy trust.

## What not to build yet

- Mandatory subscriptions, trials, seat meters, or usage meters.
- An in-app ad SDK.
- A data-broker or category-total sales pipeline.
- Paywalls around Schedule, Insights, encryption, exports, or backups.
- Bank aggregation before a prepaid customer justifies its cost and risk.
- A large enterprise platform before a real B2B buyer identifies the requirement.

## Risks and safeguards

- **Trust risk:** Keep privacy language precise and disclose operational plaintext metadata.
- **Payment/legal risk:** Review Malaysia business-income, payment, tax, affiliate, and consumer obligations before regular commercial operation.
- **Deliverability risk:** Use a verified sender for transactional email and do not mix promotional blasts into reminder infrastructure.
- **License risk:** Confirm that self-hosting, white-label, and commercial deployment terms are explicit in the proprietary license and B2B contracts.
- **Security risk:** Publish a threat model, vulnerability-reporting process, and independent review before selling privacy-critical deployments.

## Exit criteria for this plan

Revisit the model only when one of these is true:

- Hosted costs exceed what tips, supporters, and safe sponsorships cover.
- At least 50 Lifetime Supporter purchases reveal a clear high-value perk.
- A qualified B2B buyer prepays for a deployment or workshop.
- Distribution is large enough to justify testing a new revenue surface.

Until then, keep the product free, keep the privacy promise intact, and validate revenue with small paid experiments rather than building speculative monetization infrastructure.
